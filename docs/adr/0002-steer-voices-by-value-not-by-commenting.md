# Steer voices by value, not by commenting lines in and out

The common live-coding practice is to comment and uncomment lines (or prefix labels with `_`) to bring voices in and out. Here the performer instead sets values in a control panel — 0 for silent, 1, 2, 3 for a voice's variants — and the voices are defined further down. Named values are harder to fumble on stage than toggling the right line among many, keep the whole performance steerable from one screen, and read clearly to an audience watching the projected code.
