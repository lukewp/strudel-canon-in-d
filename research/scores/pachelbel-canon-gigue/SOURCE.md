# Pachelbel: Canon and Gigue in D (P.37) — score sources

Two copies of Max Seiffert's edition. **Transcribe from the 1929 scan (1)**: it is the edition itself, and public domain. Use Matmati's engraving (2) to read passages the scan makes hard to see; where they differ, the scan wins.

## 1. Seiffert's edition, 1929 (the authority)

- **File:** `IMSLP44549-PMLP04611-Pachelbel-KanonundGigueFS.pdf` (11 pages): full score for three violins, cello and cembalo
- **From:** https://imslp.org/wiki/File:PMLP04611-Pachelbel-KanonundGigueFS.pdf — scanned by Carolus (2009-10-30); downloaded by Luke, 2 Nov 2026
- **Edition:** *Organum*, Reihe III (Kammermusik), Nr. 24, edited by Max Seiffert (1868–1948), with his continuo realization. Leipzig/Köln: Kistner & Siegel, n.d. [1929], plate 28367
- **Licence:** Public Domain, per its IMSLP file page (checked by Luke, 2 Nov 2026). The print's "Alle Rechte vorbehalten" notice dates from 1929 and has lapsed: Seiffert died in 1948.
- **Seiffert's sources** (his note, page 2): an old set of parts in the Staatliche Akademie für Kirchen- und Schulmusik, Berlin-Charlottenburg (H. 8332); the urtext is in *Archiv für Musikwissenschaft* I, p. 271 (G. Beckmann).
- **Cembalo:** Seiffert's own realization of the figured bass — a ready-made source for chord and pad voices.

## What's in it (tempo and dynamic markings are Seiffert's, not Pachelbel's)

- **Kanon:** 4/4, *Sostenuto* ♩ = 56, 57 bars; the cello's ground bass repeats every 2 bars; *rall. poco a poco* from bar 55 to the end.
- **Gigue:** 12/8, *Scherzando* ♩. = 100, in two repeated halves.
